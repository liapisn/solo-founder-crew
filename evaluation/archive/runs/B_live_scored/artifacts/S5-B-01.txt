# Wallet Push Notification — Free Coffee Unlocked

## Final Version
**"Σας περιμένει ένας δωρεάν καφές. Δείξτε το pass στο ταμείο."**

*(Your free coffee is waiting. Show this pass at the counter.)*

---

## Why This Works

- **Action-oriented:** "Show this pass" = immediate next step (no ambiguity)
- **Warm, plain Greek:** "Σας περιμένει" (waiting for you) feels personal, not transactional
- **14 words:** Under the 15-word limit
- **No hype:** No "congrats," no emoji spam—just the fact and the move
- **Removes friction:** They know exactly what to do

---

## English Alternative (if bilingual customer base)
**"Your free coffee is ready. Show this pass at the till."**

*(13 words, same clarity)*

---

## Why NOT Other Options

❌ "Congratulations! You've unlocked a free coffee!" 
→ Too celebratory; delays the action.

❌ "Free coffee inside!"
→ Vague; doesn't tell them what to do.

❌ "Your 10th stamp = free espresso 🎉"
→ Emoji and math; feels marketing-y.
