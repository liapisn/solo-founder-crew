# Chunky Cookie Bar — Loyalty Pass Copy

## Primary Text (Headline)
**"Buy 9 coffees, get your 10th free"**
*(13 words)*

## Secondary Text (Subtitle)
**"Collect stamps. Enjoy your reward."**
*(5 words)*

---

## Why This Works

- **Lead with the outcome:** Free coffee, not "loyalty program"
- **Plain math:** "9 + 1 free" is instant clarity
- **Action verb:** "Collect" and "Enjoy" feel like progress, not a chore
- **Wallet-native:** Fits the small screen; reads fast in-pocket
- **Greek-friendly phrasing:** "Collect stamps" mirrors how Greeks talk about loyalty cards (σφραγίδες)

---

## Alt Version (If you want to emphasize the ease)
**"Stamp 10 coffees, get one free. That's it."**
*(10 words)*

*(Adds warmth and reassurance that there's no hidden complexity.)*

---

## On the Actual Pass Bar
Show **9 of 10 stamps filled** (visual progress bar). One empty box left. Customers *see* how close they are—that's the hook.
