## Full Pass Layout (if secondary is shown)

---

**Total: 18 words**

---

## Why This Works

- **Clarity:** No ambiguity about the reward (not points, not discounts—free coffee)
- **Action:** "Collect a stamp" is concrete, visual, and familiar to Greek café culture
- **Brevity:** Respects wallet pass space constraints
- **Warmth:** Conversational, not corporate
- **Scannable:** Headline answer + one instruction = done

The pass itself carries the visual stamp progression (iOS/Android native), so copy doesn't need to repeat it.
