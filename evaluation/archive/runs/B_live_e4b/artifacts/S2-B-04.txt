# Chunky Cookie Bar — Wallet Pass Copy

## Primary Text (Stamp Card)

**EL:**
> Κάθε καφές, ένα σφραγίδι. Το 10ο δωρεάν.

**EN:**
> Every coffee, one stamp. The 10th is free.

---

## Secondary Text (If space allows — ~15 words max)

**EL:**
> Δείξε το πάσο σου στο ταμείο.

**EN:**
> Show this pass at checkout.

---

## Backup Version (If even shorter needed — under 10 words)

**EL:**
> 10 καφέδες = 1 δωρεάν

**EN:**
> 10 coffees = 1 free

---

## Notes
- Uses numbers over spelled-out words (saves characters, scans faster on phone)
- Action is clear and immediate ("δείξε το πάσο")
- No emojis or hype—wallets render them differently across devices
- Works in Apple/Google Wallet preview (which truncates aggressively)
