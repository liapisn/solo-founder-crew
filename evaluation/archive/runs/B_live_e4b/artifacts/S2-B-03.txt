# Chunky Cookie Bar — Loyalty Pass Copy

## Primary Text (Stamp Card)
**Greek:**  
Κάθε καφές μετράει. Ο 10ος είναι δικός σου.

**English:**  
Every coffee counts. Your 10th is free.

---

## Secondary Line (if space allows — optional)
**Greek:**  
Συγκέντρωσε 10 σφραγίδες.

**English:**  
Collect 10 stamps.

---

## Notes
- **Word count:** 10 words (Greek), 6 words (English)
- **Tone:** Direct, warm. No fluff.
- **What it does:** Names the reward (free coffee), explains the mechanic (stamps), removes ambiguity.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Primary text should be large; stamps/progress tracker visual does the heavy lifting.
- **Compliance:** No personal data referenced; no urgency tricks.
