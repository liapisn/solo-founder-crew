# Chunky Cookie Bar — Wallet Pass Copy

## Primary (Front of Pass)

**Headline:**
> Buy 9, get 1 free

**Subheading:**
> Every coffee. Collect stamps in-store.

---

## Secondary (Back of Pass / Fine Print)

**Redemption instruction:**
> Show this pass at checkout. One free coffee when complete.

---

## Why This Works

- **Headline (4 words)**: Outcome first, concrete, no jargon.
- **Subheading (6 words)**: Removes friction ("in-store" = no app confusion, no scanning codes).
- **Fine print (9 words)**: One sentence, one action. Removes ambiguity at redemption.
- **Total: 19 words** — leaves room for Apple/Google Wallet layout, readable on small screens.
- **Tone**: Warm peer (not "loyalty member" corporate speak).
- **Greek equivalent** (if bilingual pass):
  > Αγόρασε 9, πάρε 1 δωρεάν
  > Κάθε καφές. Συγκέντρωσε σφραγίδες στο κατάστημα.

---

## Design Principle (Copy + Layout)

The pass should feel like a friendly agreement, not a contract. Chunky Cookie Bar's brand (warmth, casual) should shine through the simplicity.
